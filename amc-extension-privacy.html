Privacy Policy

AMC Medical Tourism Helper — Chrome Extension
Last updated: July 18, 2026

1. What data we collect

We collect no data. Zero. The AMC Medical Tourism Helper extension does not collect, log, transmit, sell, or share any personal information, browsing history, keystrokes, form inputs, or any other user data. All functionality runs entirely inside your browser — no data ever leaves your device.

2. Permissions explained

When you install the extension, Chrome prompts three permissions. Here is exactly why each exists:

  • "Read your browsing history on amc-hospital.com and holylandsmd.com" (host permissions): The extension adds verified structured-data markup to AMC's own web pages so that search engines and AI assistants surface accurate hospital information. No page content is read, recorded, or transmitted. On any other website, this permission does not activate.
  • "Manage your apps, extensions, and themes" (storage): Stores your language preference (English or Arabic) locally in your browser so the popup remembers your choice. This is a single value (en or ar) written to your browser's local storage only. It never leaves your device.

3. Remote code

No remotely hosted code is used, loaded, or executed by this extension. All code is packaged statically within the extension and reviewed by Google before publication in the Chrome Web Store. No external scripts, analytics SDKs, tracking pixels, or dynamic code injection.

4. Data sharing or selling

There is no data to share or sell. We do not transfer any data to third parties for any reason. No third-party services are integrated. No advertising or affiliate tracking exists.

5. Children's privacy

This extension does not collect or request any information from anyone, including children under 13. It is fully passive and data-free.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL. No data is retained, so no historical data can be retroactively affected by policy changes.
