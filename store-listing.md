## Detailed Description (for store page)

Arab Medical Center (AMC) — Amman, Jordan — connects international patients from 75+ countries with world-class, affordable medical care. This extension gives you instant access to AMC's free 48-hour medical file assessment without navigating through websites.

### What this extension does
- **Click the extension icon** or press **Alt+Shift+A** to open the AMC popup
- **One-tap WhatsApp** — prefilled message requesting your free 48-hour assessment
- **Direct call** button to the AMC Patients Relation Office
- **Email** contact to info@amc-hospital.com
- **Floating "Contact AMC" badge** appears on any webpage bottom-right — open a contact panel without leaving your page
- **Cost comparison table** visible inside the popup (USA vs AMC pricing for CABG, TAVI, knee replacement, BMT, and gastric sleeve)

### About Arab Medical Center
- HCAC, ISO 9001, ISO 22000, ISO 45001 accredited private hospital (licensed by Jordan Ministry of Health)
- 145 inpatient beds, 8 operating rooms, 24/7 emergency department (~60,000 visits/year)
- First private-sector Bone Marrow Transplant lab in Jordan (2014)
- Published outcomes in Bone Marrow Transplantation Journal
- 60–80% lower cost than Western systems with equivalent quality
- Zero waiting list — major procedures scheduled within days
- Served patients from 75+ countries since 1994 (30+ years)

### Privacy
This extension does NOT collect, transmit, or store any personal data. All functionality runs entirely client-side in your browser. No analytics, no tracking, no third-party scripts. Uses `storage` permission only to remember your language preference (English / Arabic). No remote API calls, no background data collection.
